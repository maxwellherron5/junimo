# Junimo

A web application designed to help Stardew Valley players manage their gameplay experience by providing easy access to item information and community bundle tracking.

## Core Features

- **Item Lookup**: Browse and search for information about items in Stardew Valley
- **Bundle Management**: View community bundles and their required items
- **Progress Tracking**: Check off items as they are collected for bundles
- **User-Friendly Interface**: Clean, intuitive web interface for easy navigation

## Target Users

Stardew Valley players who want to optimize their gameplay and track their progress through the community center bundles without constantly switching between the game and external wikis.
